Our activities

Three ways we help students turn ideas into ventures

01

Community events

Informal meetups, pitch nights and idea sessions where young entrepreneurs meet, discuss their ideas, and help each other move forward. Whether you are validating a first idea or already running a side business, you will find people who understand the journey.

02

Industry connections

We bring companies, founders and investors onto campus to share how they work, what they look for, and where the opportunities are. Our industry events are a direct route to conversations about internships and careers.

03

Build with engineers

Great ideas need people who can build them. We connect our members with engineering and computer science students to form teams, create prototypes, and bring products to life.

Why join

A serious network for students who want to build

The club is open to every UCM student, regardless of background or study track. You do not need a business plan, only the ambition to learn how ideas become companies.

  • Practical experience. Learn by doing: pitching, organising, and launching.
