Our first community event of the academic year is an informal networking evening for anyone interested in entrepreneurship.

## Programme

1. **Welcome** and an introduction to the club and its plans for the year
2. **Lightning pitches**: one-minute introductions of ideas and projects, open to anyone who wants to share
3. **Open networking** with drinks

## Who should come?

Everyone. You do not need an idea to attend. Many of the best teams form when someone with an idea meets someone with the skills to help build it.

*Location and time will be shared with members by email. Not a member yet? [Get in touch](/contact/).*
