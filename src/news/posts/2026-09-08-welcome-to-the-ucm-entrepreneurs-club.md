We are proud to introduce the **UCM Entrepreneurs Club**, a student organisation for everyone at University College Maastricht who wants to build something of their own.

## Why a club for entrepreneurs?

Starting something new is hard, and it is harder alone. Students with ambitious ideas often lack three things: peers to test their thinking against, access to people in industry, and collaborators with the technical skills to build a product. Our club exists to bring those three together.

## What to expect this year

- **Community events** where you can meet other aspiring founders, pitch ideas and get honest feedback.
- **Industry sessions** with companies from Maastricht and beyond, including conversations about internships.
- **Builder matchmaking** connecting founders with engineering and computer science students.

## Get involved

Whether you have a fully formed business plan or just a curiosity about how start-ups work, you are welcome. Keep an eye on this page for upcoming events, or [contact us](/contact/) to get involved.
