An idea only becomes a business once it is built. That is why we are launching the **Builders Programme**, which connects our members with engineering and computer science students.

## How it works

- **Founders** present a short description of their idea and the skills they are looking for.
- **Builders** browse the ideas and join the projects that interest them.
- **Teams** meet throughout the semester and present their progress at the end of term.

## For engineering and CS students

The programme is a chance to work on real products, gain experience outside the classroom, and possibly become a co-founder. No prior start-up experience is needed.

Interested in joining as a founder or a builder? [Email us](/contact/) and we will send you the details.
